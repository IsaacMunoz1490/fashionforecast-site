How it works

From your closet to today's forecast.

Step 1

Snap your closet.

Photograph your clothes or import outfit photos. AI tags each piece with its category, warmth and style.

Step 2

Live weather for your exact neighborhood, including SF micro-climates. Three outfits from what you own, and a reason for each.

Step 3

Pack for any city. Pro

Tell it where and when. It builds a small capsule and an outfit for every day of the trip.

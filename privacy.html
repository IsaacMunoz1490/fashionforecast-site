Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-10-19  ·  Fashion Forecast for iPhone

Fashion Forecast is a wardrobe app built with your privacy as a design constraint, not an afterthought. This policy explains exactly what data leaves your phone, what stays on it, and why.

1. What we collect

Fashion Forecast stores the following on your device only:

  • Clothing photos you add
  • Item details (category, warmth, material, style)
  • Your wear history and laundry tracking
  • Trip plans and packing lists
  • App settings and wardrobe tab preferences

We never receive, access, or store any of this. It does not leave your iPhone.

2. What we do not collect

We collect no account information, no name, no email address, no gender. We use no advertising identifiers (IDFA), no analytics services that transmit data off your device, and no crash-reporting services that send data to us.

3. Location

Location is used only in the moment to fetch current weather for outfit suggestions. It is never stored on your device, never sent to us, and never shared with third parties beyond the weather APIs listed below. Your coordinates are rounded to approximately 1 km before being sent to Open-Meteo.

4. Third-party services

The app contacts two external services. We do not sell or share your data with any of them beyond what is described here.

  • Open-Meteo — receives rounded location coordinates and the city names you enter for trips, to return weather forecasts.
  • Google Gemini — used only when you choose AI import and enter your own free Gemini API key. The photos you select are sent directly to Google under your key to identify the garments and fill in their details; this is the only time your photos leave your device, and only for the photos you choose to import. Fashion Forecast has no server of its own and never receives or stores them, and the results are saved on your device. Google processes them under its own terms for the key you use — on Google's free tier, Google may use this content to improve its products and a reviewer may see it, so only import photos you are comfortable sending. Google's Privacy Policy governs that data.
When you use AI import, your photos go to Google — not to us. You must be 18 or older to use AI import. You can use the app entirely without it; manual photo entry and editing never send any photos off your device.

5. Photos and EXIF metadata

Clothing photos are stored only on your device. When photos are imported, the app re-saves them in a way that strips GPS location metadata (EXIF data). We do not read, retain, or transmit location information embedded in photos.

6. Backups

You can export an encrypted backup file and save it to a location you control — the Files app or iCloud Drive. We never receive backup files. If you choose password encryption, the password never leaves your device. We cannot recover it.

7. Face ID and app lock

The optional Face ID lock uses iOS's built-in authentication. Fashion Forecast never sees or stores your biometric data. Apple handles the authentication entirely.

8. Children

Fashion Forecast is intended only for people 18 and older. It is not directed to anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 has sent us information (for example, by emailing support), contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We will update this page and notify users inside the app before making any material changes — specifically before adding any account system or cloud storage. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at hello.fashionforecast@gmail.com.
